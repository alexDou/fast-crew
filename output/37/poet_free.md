White hull heeling, sail of gold unfurled,  
Battling clouds that scarf the sky’s wide dome.  
Ten red jackets labor, a clenched world  
On decks that tilt like Atlases turned foam.  

Lines whip taut where calloused fingers bind,  
Chanting winds through rigging’s braided hymn,  
While waves gnash ivory at rails inclined—  
A pact of salt between the tide and limb.  

At stern, one grips the rope with pilgrim’s creed,  
At bow, another leans—his spine a bow—  
Both tethered to the winch, the stanchion’s need,  
And land’s blue ghost where straining helmsmen go.  

What is the sea but motion’s hungry throne?  
They sail its pulse—each lurching deck their own.
