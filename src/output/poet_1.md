**Twilight's Mirror**

The sun descends in amber fire,
Painting sky with soft desire,
Orange, pink, and purple blend—
Where the day and night transcend.

Silhouettes of trees stand tall,
Guarding beauty's graceful call,
On the tranquil water's face,
Ripples dance with gentle grace.

Vivid colors gently spill,
Reflected on the mirrored still,
Peace descends like evening dew,
Nature's masterpiece in view.

All the world grows calm and deep,
As the glowing colors seep
Into hearts that pause to see—
Twilight's gift of harmony.
