Sunset’s Hush  

Amber skies dissolve in liquid gold,  
Where ripples weave the dusk’s reflected gleam.  
Silhouetted trees in twilight hold  
Their vigil o’er the lake, a whispered dream.  

The water blushes pink, then purple deep,  
A canvas brushed by heaven’s gentle hand.  
The world suspends—no stir, no leap—  
As shadows stretch across the tranquil land.  

Let silence cloak you in its soft embrace,  
Where sky and earth in molten colors blend.  
This peace, unchained by time or space,  
Is sunset’s gift—where daylight meets its end.
